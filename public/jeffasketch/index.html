Give it a spin.

One line, endless possibilities. The pencil stays down as you explore.

  • Keyboard: use the arrow keys or WASD. Hold two keys for diagonals, or Shift to move slowly.
  • Mouse: drag the left knob left/right, or the right knob up/down. You can combine dragging with the keyboard.
  • Touch: slide each knob along its axis. Use a finger on each knob to draw diagonally.
  • Fresh canvas: Shake to clear, with 10 seconds to restore.
  • Challenges: trace the outline, then tap Done. Take your time—there’s no timer.
